SPEED TIME DISTANCE:

Formula:
    > DISTANCE = SPEED * TIME
    > Avg. speed = Total Distance/Total Time    
    > km/h * 5/18 = m/s

Questions:
    Q. The speed of a bus is 54 km/hr without stoppage and 45 km/hr with stoppage. For how many minutes does the bus stops per hour?
    A. Difference = 9km/h
        Now Time = D/S = 9/(first distance) = (9/54)*60 = 10min

Train Questions:
    1. Train passing a pole/tree will also cover the distance of its own length, so distance = length of train.
    2. Train passing a station will have distance of train + station

Relative Speed:
    > In same direction speeds is subtracted
    > In opposite direction speed is added

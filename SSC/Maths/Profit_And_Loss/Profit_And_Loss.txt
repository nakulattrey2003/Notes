PROFIT AND LOSS:

Profit = SP − CP
Loss = CP − SP

Profit% = Profit/CP × 100
Loss% = Loss/CP × 100

SP (profit) = CP * (1 + Profit%/100)
SP (loss) = CP * (1 - Loss%/100)

CP (profit) = SP / (1 + Profit%/100)
CP (loss) = SP / (1 - Loss%/100)

Note: When Loss% and Profit% is same then CP=(SP1+SP2)/2

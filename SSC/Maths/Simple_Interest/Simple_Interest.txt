SIMPLE INTEREST:

SI = PRT/100
A = P + I

P = Principle / Initial Sum / Invested Sum / Certain Sum
I/SI = Interest / Simple Interest
T = Time
A = Amount

Agar aap bank mein 1000Rs deposit karte ho or 1 saal baad withdraw karte ho or bank aapko 1200Rs deta hai to
P = 1000, T = 1, A = 1200, I = 200

Trick: If in the question it is mentioned that P is 'x' times then I is (x-1)*100%

Note: Examlple ke lie - Agar question me %rate 20% dia hai or years 2 years and 6 months dia hai to 2 years ke lie %rate 20% hi rahega but 6 months ke Interest (6/12)*20% = 10% ho jaega

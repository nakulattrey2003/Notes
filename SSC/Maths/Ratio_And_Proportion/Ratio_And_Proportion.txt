        a b c d

        _______
        48:72:90:105
        16:24:30:35 = ans

    2. Mean Proportion:
        a or b ka mean proportion hoga root(a * b)
        logic = suppose mean proportion = y so (a/y) should be equal to (y/b)
        Eg. find mean proportion of 25 and 9
        Ans is root(25*9) = 15

    3. Third Proportion:    
        a or b ka third proportion hoga b^2/a
        logic = suppose mean proportion = y so (a/b) should be equal to (b/y)
        Eg. find third proportion of 36 and 12
        Ans is 4

    4. Fourth Proportion:
        a, b, c ka Fourth proportion hoga bc/a
        logic = suppose mean proportion = y so (a/b) should be equal to (c/y)
        Eg. find Fourth proportion of 4, 9 and 12
        Ans is 27

    5. Duplicate Ratio:
        a or b ka Duplicate ratio hoga a^2, b^2
        Eg. find Duplicate ratio of 7 and 8
        Ans is 49, 64

    6. Sub Duplicate Ratio:
        a or b ka Sub Duplicate ratio hoga root(a), root(b)
        Eg. find Duplicate ratio of 121 and 64
        Ans is 11, 8

    7. Triplicate Ratio:
        a or b ka Triplicate ratio hoga a^3, b^3
        Eg. find Duplicate ratio of 2 and 4
        Ans is 8, 64

    8. Sub Triplicate Ratio:
        a or b ka Sub Triplicate Ratio hoga cuberoot(a), cuberoot(b)
        Eg. find Duplicate ratio of 27, 1331
        Ans is 3, 11

> Mixtures:
    Type 1: Same Ratio:
        Eg. Two alloys contains gold and copper in ratio 4 : 5 and 1 : 5 respectively. If
        these two alloys are mixed to form a third alloy in same ratio then find ratio of
        gold and copper in new alloy.
        => Agar question me same ratio bola hai to uska matlab 1:1 hai
        dono ratio ko ek saath likh lena (meaning quantities ko equal karenge)
                    G   C
        Alloy 1:    4   5  sum = 9
        Alloy 2:    1   5  sum = 6
        dono ke sum ke lcm leke multiply karenge
                    G   C  LCM = 18
        Alloy 1:    8   10
        Alloy 2:    3   15
                   +______
        Sum:        11  25 = ans
    
    Type 2: Different Ratio:
        Two jar contains milk and water in ratio 5 : 7 and 8 : 7. If these two jar are
        mixed in ratio 2 : 3 then find the ratio of milk and water in new mixture?
        => Agar question me same ratio bola hai to uska matlab 1:1 hai
        dono ratio ko ek saath likh lena (meaning quantities ko equal karenge)
                M   W
        Jar 1:  5   7  sum = 12
        Jar 2:  8   7  sum = 15
        dono ke sum ke lcm leke multiply karenge or kyonki 2:3 me add karna hai to  ko 2 se or W ko 3 se multiply karenge
                M       W  LCM = 60
        Jar 1:  5*5*2   7*5*3
        Jar 2:  8*4*2   7*4*3
               +_____________
        Sum:    146     154
        Ans:     73      77

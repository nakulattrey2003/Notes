Simlification:
    > Square: 
        Case 1:
            Normal Number:
            Kisi number ka square karna hai to unit digit wale ka square karna hai(6) or uske aage ke number ka square karna hai(..5)
            Dono ko saath me rakhna hai to die hue number ko aapas me multiply karne ke baad 20 se multiply add karna hai or add karna hai.
            Eg 56^2
            5^2  6^2  (5*6*2)
            2536
            +600
            ----
            3136
            

        Case 2: 
            Number jiske unit digit me 5 ho
            Pahle unit digit ke 5 ka square karna hai baaki bache hue number ko +1 karke multiply karna hai(4*(4+1))
            or fir dono ko saath me rakhdena hai
            Eg 65^2
            6*7  5^2  
            = 4225

    > Square Root:
        Case 1:
            Perfect Roots:
            Die hue number ke last ke 2 digits ko alag rakhna hai or aage ki baaki ko alag
            Fir wo number likhne hai jinka square karne se unit digit match ho jae.
            Fir wo number likhna hai jiske square karne ke baad jo root me aage ka bacha hua number tha wo usse bada ho.
            ab (wo number) * (wo number + 1) karne ke baad root me bacha hua number bada hai to (wo number + 1) aega warna (wo number) aega.
            Eg.
            Root(7056)
                = 70/56
                    6 | 4  
                    70 number 8^2 se bada hai to 8*9 karenge
                    ab kyonki 8*9 = 72 number 70 se bada hai isliye 86 aega agar chota ya equal hota to 84 ata

        Case 2:
            Non Perfect Roots:
            Jin numbers ka unit digit 2, 3, 7, 8 hota hai wo sab non-perfect hote hai
            Solve karne ka tarika ->
                root(c) = root(a+-b) = root(a)+-(b/2*root(a))
                Ex. root(60) = root(64-4) = root(64)-(4/2*root(64)) = 8.25

    > Cube Root:
        Die hue number ke last 3 digits ko saath me rakhna hai baaki bache hue ko saath mein
        Check karna hai konse number ke cube ka unit digit given number ke unit digit se match hota hai or usko likh lena hai.
        Bache hue number ko dekhna hai ki wo konse cube ke beech mein ata hai or chote wale number ko likh lena hai.
        Ex: root3(1860867)
            1860/867
                 3 ka cube 27 hota hai, unit digit match ho gaya or 1860 12 or 13 ke cube ke beech me ata hai to yahan se 12 lelenge
                 ans = 123

    > Multiplication:
        Jo bhi number dia hai usko base ke paas lear ao. Base jaise 100, 200, 500
        Jis number jo jitna bhi chota karoge calculation utni asaan hogi.
        Ex. 106*212
        = (100 + 6) * 212
        = 21200 + 1272
        = 22472

    > Divisibility Rule:
        By 2: Last digit is even (0, 2, 4, 6, 8).
        By 3: Sum of digits is divisible by 3.
        By 4: Number formed by last two digits is divisible by 4.
        By 5: Last digit is 0 or 5.
        By 6: Number is divisible by both 2 and 3.
        By 7:
            Identify the last digit and double it.
            Subtract this doubled value from the rest of the number.
            Check if the difference is 0, a multiple of 7, or apply the rule again
            to the difference.
        By 8: Number formed by last three digits is divisible by 8.
        By 9: Sum of digits is divisible by 9.
        By 10: Last digit is 0
        By 11: 
            Take (sum of digits in odd positions) - (sum of digits in even
            positions); if result is 0 or a multiple of 11, the number is divisible
            by 11.
